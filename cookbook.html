Zoe's cookbook

Before More Than a Meal, Zoe co-wrote A-Z Recipes with Aaron Silver for her 2023 senior experience project: 20 original recipes, from appetizers to dessert.

Food is love

"As two avid food lovers, we have taken our time over the past few weeks to craft a cookbook with love. All of our ingredients are locally sourced, and all recipes are our own, original recipes."

"Our journey to craft 6 of the best entrees was inspired by our family. Both of our parents are wonders in the kitchen ... These recipes come from the heart, and most importantly, have been granted the mom seal of approval."

From the introduction to A-Z Recipes

That same idea, cooking with local ingredients and sharing it with the people you love, is what More Than a Meal is built on.
