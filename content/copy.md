## 2. Our story

**Page heading**
Our story

**Paragraph 1**
A lot of people hit a wall in second year. You move into your first apartment, the dining hall is gone, and you realize you've never really cooked for yourself. I wanted to help with that. Learning to cook is a big part of building a healthy routine, and it's a lot easier when you're doing it with people you like.

**Paragraph 2**
For me, cooking has always been about bringing people together. So I started More Than a Meal: cooking classes for students, by students. You and your friends cook together, learn a few go-to recipes, and leave with your meal prep for the week.

**Paragraph 3**
We cook with fresh produce from farms here in Charlottesville, so you get to know where your food comes from. And part of every class goes to fighting food insecurity in our community.

**Signature**
Zoe, founder

### One Pot Lemon Chicken Orzo
PHOTO: IMG_8431

Seared chicken thighs, toasted orzo, zucchini and peppers, baked in one pot with lemon and Boursin cheese. Serves 4 and doubles easily, with only one pot to wash.

$14 per person · groups of 6+

Tags: Alcohol-free option

Small print: Contains wheat and dairy. The white wine can be swapped for more chicken broth.

---

### White Bean Chicken Chili
PHOTO: none yet

A creamy chili with ground chicken, white beans, corn, green chilis and lime, finished with a topping bar. Serves 6 to 8 and freezes well, so it lasts you the whole week.

$14 per person · groups of 6+

Tags: Gluten free*, Freezes well

Small print: *Check your broth label, and skip the bread or use gluten-free bread. Contains dairy.

---

### Marry Me Chicken
PHOTO: none yet

Golden chicken breasts baked in a creamy sauce with sun-dried tomatoes, zucchini, Parmesan and fresh basil. The recipe makes four chicken breasts. Serve it with pasta or potatoes.

$14 per person · groups of 6+

Tags: Gluten free*

Small print: *Check your broth label, and serve with potatoes instead of pasta. Contains dairy.

---

### Burrito Bowls
PHOTO: none yet

Build-your-own bowls with cilantro lime rice, seasoned black beans, chicken or ground beef, fajita veggies, homemade guacamole and corn salsa. Make each part once and build bowls all week.

$14 per person · groups of 6+

Tags: Gluten free*, Vegetarian option, Dairy-free option

Small print: *Check the labels on your salsa and hot sauce. For vegetarian, skip the meat. For dairy-free, skip the cheese and sour cream.

---

### Vodka-less Pasta
PHOTO: none yet

A creamy tomato pasta with garlic, onion, Parmesan and a little red pepper heat. Ready in about 20 minutes and serves 4, so you can double it for the week.

$14 per person · groups of 6+

Tags: Vegetarian*, Alcohol-free

Small print: Contains wheat and dairy. *Most Parmesan is made with animal rennet. Use a vegetarian hard cheese if that matters to you.
