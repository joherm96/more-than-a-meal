Classes

Every class is hands-on and beginner-friendly. Pick a recipe, bring your group, and go home with food for the week.

$14 per person · groups of 6+

One Pot Lemon Chicken Orzo

Seared chicken thighs, toasted orzo, zucchini and peppers, baked in one pot with lemon and Boursin cheese. Serves 4 and doubles easily, with only one pot to wash.

Contains wheat and dairy. The white wine can be swapped for more chicken broth.

$14 per person · groups of 6+

White Bean Chicken Chili

A creamy chili with ground chicken, white beans, corn, green chilis and lime, finished with a topping bar. Serves 6 to 8 and freezes well, so it lasts you the whole week.

*Check your broth label, and skip the bread or use gluten-free bread. Contains dairy.

$14 per person · groups of 6+

Marry Me Chicken

Golden chicken breasts baked in a creamy sauce with sun-dried tomatoes, zucchini, Parmesan and fresh basil. The recipe makes four chicken breasts. Serve it with pasta or potatoes.

*Check your broth label, and serve with potatoes instead of pasta. Contains dairy.

$14 per person · groups of 6+

Burrito Bowls

Build-your-own bowls with cilantro lime rice, seasoned black beans, chicken or ground beef, fajita veggies, homemade guacamole and corn salsa. Make each part once and build bowls all week.

*Check the labels on your salsa and hot sauce. For vegetarian, skip the meat. For dairy-free, skip the cheese and sour cream.

$14 per person · groups of 6+

Book this class: Burrito Bowls

Vodka-less Pasta

A creamy tomato pasta with garlic, onion, Parmesan and a little red pepper heat. Ready in about 20 minutes and serves 4, so you can double it for the week.

Contains wheat and dairy. *Most Parmesan is made with animal rennet. Use a vegetarian hard cheese if that matters to you.

$14 per person · groups of 6+

Book this class: Vodka-less Pasta

Hot Honey Shrimp

Sweet and spicy honey garlic shrimp over rice with roasted veggies. It comes together in about 20 minutes, so it's an easy one to make every week for meal prep.

*Check your hot sauce or chili crisp label. Contains shellfish.
