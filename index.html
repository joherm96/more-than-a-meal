Book a class

Learn to cook with friends. Leave with meals for the week.

UVA students teach hands-on meal prep classes using fresh produce from Charlottesville farms. Part of every class goes to the UVA Community Food Pantry to help fight food insecurity.

Book a class

How it works

  1. Get your group together.

    Friends, a club, your sorority. Six people or more.

  2. Pick a recipe.

    Choose from our class menu.

  3. Cook together.

    A student teacher walks you through it, step by step.

  4. Take it home.

    You leave with your meal prep for the week.
