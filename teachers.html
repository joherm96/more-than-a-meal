Meet the founders and teachers

We are the UVA students who started More Than a Meal together, and we teach every class.

Zoe

I started More Than a Meal because a lot of second-years move into their first apartment without ever having cooked for themselves. For me, cooking has always been about bringing people together, so we built classes for students, by students.

Anneke

Loves to cook: Seasonal, Mediterranean-inspired dishes made with fresh, local ingredients.

I'm a third-year at UVA studying Public Policy and Environmental Thought and Practice. I've worked on two educational farms, and I care a lot about sustainable food systems, connecting people with where their food comes from, and bringing communities together through cooking.

Meg

Loves to cook: The recipes my mom taught me growing up, especially lemon chicken orzo and chicken chili.

I'm a second-year at UVA studying Foreign Affairs and Media Studies. I love cooking and sharing that love with others, and I'm so excited to bring More Than a Meal to life.

Lauren Chesley

Loves to cook: Buffalo chicken dip, vodka pasta, gochujang fried chicken and loaded nachos.

I grew up with celiac disease and found my love for cooking by recreating dishes I saw online or always wanted to try but couldn't usually find gluten-free at restaurants. What started as a necessity turned into a passion for trying new recipes and exploring different cuisines.

Maddie

I'm a second-year at UVA, and I grew up working in restaurants. My dad owned a wood-fired pizza food truck and passed his love for food down to me. I'm looking forward to sharing what I've learned with my peers.

Cooking classes for students, by students. Charlottesville, VA.
