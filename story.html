Our story

A lot of people hit a wall in second year. You move into your first apartment, the dining hall is gone, and you realize you've never really cooked for yourself. I wanted to help with that. Learning to cook is a big part of building a healthy routine, and it's a lot easier when you're doing it with people you like.

For me, cooking has always been about bringing people together. So I started More Than a Meal: cooking classes for students, by students. You and your friends cook together, learn a few go-to recipes, and leave with your meal prep for the week.

We cook with fresh produce from farms here in Charlottesville, so you get to know where your food comes from. And part of every class goes to the UVA Community Food Pantry (opens in a new tab), to help fight food insecurity in our community.

Zoe, founder
