Farm partners

Our partner is the Morven Kitchen Garden, a student-run garden on UVA's Morven Farm. Buying local means you know where your food comes from.

Morven Kitchen Garden

Morven Kitchen Garden

Morven Farm, 600 Morven Drive, Charlottesville

Our partner. The garden covers 1.5 acres of growing land on UVA's Morven Farm and is a shared project between Morven and UVA students. It has been growing food sustainably for over 10 years and uses no synthetic pesticides or herbicides.

What grows there: more than 40 kinds of fruits, vegetables and flowers that draw helpful birds and insects. There are also mushroom logs, three beehives and a chicken coop.

Where it goes: since 2017 the garden has sold produce to UVA Dining for the dining halls. It also donates to local nonprofits working on food insecurity in Charlottesville. Students also started a CSA there in 2011, where shareholders get a weekly bag of produce.

Get involved: semester and summer internships, weekly volunteer hours, class visits and garden tours.

More local food at UVA

Morven is our partner. These other student gardens on Grounds are also worth a visit.

UVA Student Garden

UVA Student Garden

Alderman and McCormick Roads, near Observatory Hill Dining Hall

Grows tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers, melons, squash, berries, herbs and native flowers, without pesticides or chemical fertilizers. Volunteers get first pick of the harvest and the rest goes to the UVA Community Food Pantry.

Read about it (UVA Today, opens in a new tab)

Hereford Garden

Hereford Residential Garden

Hereford Residential College

Grows tomatoes, garlic, onions, peppers, eggplants and potatoes, plus a small orchard of plums, apples, blueberries, pawpaws and elderberries. It is also the classroom for a one-credit course called The Garden Party.
