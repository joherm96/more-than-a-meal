Farm partners

We cook with fresh produce from farms around Charlottesville. Buying local means you know where your food comes from, and your class supports the people who grow it.

Morven Kitchen Garden

UVA's Morven Farm, about 15 minutes from downtown Charlottesville

A one-acre, student-run market garden on UVA's Morven Farm. It grows more than 40 varieties of fruits, vegetables, herbs and mushrooms, keeps honeybees, and donates produce to local nonprofits fighting food insecurity.
